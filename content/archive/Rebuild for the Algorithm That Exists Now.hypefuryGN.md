# Rebuild for the Algorithm That Exists Now

## The Story

By 2024 Abby Rawlinson had already used Instagram to fill a private practice, launch courses, and land a Penguin book deal. Then the platform turned on her. On the sales page for her *Instagram Growth Kit* she describes the drop plainly: "But in 2024, something shifted. Posts that would once have taken off suddenly flopped. Engagement dropped. Reach became unpredictable." For a creator whose whole business sat on one account, that is an existential problem, not a bad week.

Her response was not to post harder or wait for the old playbook to come back. She rebuilt the method. She frames the rebuild as a fusion of two things that are usually treated as rivals: the timeless fundamentals and the current mechanics. In her words the reset "combines the foundations that have always mattered — trust, resonance, specificity — with what's actually working on Instagram right now." The fundamentals kept the account recognizably hers; the current-format tactics got it back into distribution.

The payoff was a reversal, not just a recovery. "Ironically, after thinking my growth had stalled, 2025 became my biggest year yet. I was gaining around 5,000 followers a month, and one new course quickly generated over £10k in passive income." The ~5,000 figure is Instagram followers per month; the £10k is revenue from a single course. The account that looked stalled in 2024 was, once rebuilt, adding followers faster than ever the following year.

What makes this a mechanism rather than a survival story is the diagnosis behind it. A reach collapse is ambiguous: it can read as your content getting worse, or the audience leaving, or a signal to quit while you are ahead. Abby read it instead as the format layer changing while her fundamentals stayed sound, and she rebuilt only that layer while protecting the fundamentals underneath. The lesson is in the separation: treat trust, resonance, and specificity as permanent, and treat the tactical surface as something you re-tune whenever the platform moves.

## Lesson for Creators

An algorithm downturn is a signal to rebuild your method, not to abandon the platform. Split your approach into two layers: the durable fundamentals (who you are for, why they trust you, what you are specific about) and the tactical surface (formats, hooks, cadence that the platform currently rewards). When reach drops, hold the fundamentals fixed and re-tune the surface to what is working now. Abby did exactly that after her 2024 collapse and turned a stalled account into her biggest growth year, adding roughly 5,000 Instagram followers a month in 2025.
