# Pinterest as an Evergreen Traffic Engine

## The Story

Olivia Wickstrom built a second front door to her Substack on a platform most newsletter writers ignore. Pinterest sent over 100,000 unique visitors to Petal + Hearth, and she insists none of it required ads, design skill, or a following, just consistency and keywords.

Her first principle is blunt: _"Pinterest can only send traffic to places that exist"_, so every pin needs a real destination. From there it is search behavior, not aesthetics: she types a topic into the Pinterest search bar, harvests the autocomplete phrases people are actually using, and works them into pin titles, descriptions, and board names. Most pins go nowhere, but a cluster of fifteen to twenty power pins keep circulating for months and carry the bulk of the traffic.

## Lesson for Creators

Pinterest is a slow search engine, not a social feed. Pin consistently against the keywords it hands you, expect most pins to flop, and let the rare evergreen winners compound into traffic long after you posted them.
