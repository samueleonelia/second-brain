# Zero-Budget Launch Through Borrowed Communities

## The Story

For a LinkedIn writing tool called Post Rewriter, Maja and the founder ran the launch with no ad spend. "how we got our first 1,000 users and 100 clients it was literally organically it was done on zero budget."

The distribution came from communities they did not have to build. They posted into Facebook groups that already held their audience, after weeks of genuinely engaging there rather than dropping a cold pitch. They also used the tool inside their own content stream, dogfooding it in public so prospects saw it working.

Only after the messaging landed and word of mouth started did they add paid-style effort: "we scale it a little bit with a cold Outreach" once they knew the persona was specific and the pitch converted. The sequence was deliberate, borrowed audience first, warm outreach second, cold outreach last.

## Lesson for Creators

You do not need a budget to find the first thousand users. Show up and add value in communities that already hold your buyers, use your own product in public as proof, and only scale to cold outreach once warm channels confirm the pitch.
