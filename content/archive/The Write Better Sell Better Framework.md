# The Write Better Sell Better Framework

## The Story

Jasmin's mantra for 13+ years: "Write better, sell better" (Source 1). His core belief: "writing is 90% psychology and 10% formatting" (Source 1).

**The Hook:** Represents 90% of a good post's effectiveness. It functions as a promise to interrupt scrollers. Strategy: answer one question, "What is the one thing I want my reader to learn?" Best format: single-line hook maximum, because LinkedIn shows approximately three lines including spaces. He avoids clickbait and delivers on the promise made (Source 1).

**The Rehook:** The second line that either adds "heft" or creates intrigue. Two methods: (1) address common objections directly, e.g., "zero dollar investment," or (2) state something opposite to the hook to create psychological curiosity. "Attention means nothing without retention" -- hooks grab attention; rehooks ensure readers finish (Source 1).

**The Signposting Technique:** Insert brief context showing how you know your information. Example: "As a CEO managing teams for 20 years, I know how exhausting 4 a.m. starts are" instead of "As a CEO, stay disciplined with morning routines." This credibility signal makes content untransferable and builds trust (Source 2).

**Formatting:** He tests pixel placement and character counts to control line breaks. Uses rhythm patterns (1-2-1 or 1-3-1 line structures) for visual interest. Optimizes for mobile scrolling (Source 1).

**Image strategy:** 4:5 aspect ratio is optimal because it takes full screen real estate. Include your face when possible. His current profile image is AI-generated using Secta Labs (Source 1).

**Pinned comments strategy:** Author comments naturally appear first for approximately 90% of readers. He uses them for behind-the-scenes content, bonus lessons (repurposed older posts), free Q&As, and calls-to-action. Key rule: keep sales pitches in comments, not in the main post (Source 2).

**The 1+3 commenting method:** Comment once as the main response, then reply three times to ideal client prospects in the comment thread (Source 2).

**Algorithm priorities:** 30+ seconds minimum dwell time signals quality. Text-only posts consistently outperform image posts on his account (Source 1, Source 2). Less than 2% of LinkedIn's 1 billion+ users post weekly, creating significant opportunity (Source 2).

## Lesson for Creators

Jasmin's framework is unusually specific. Not "write good hooks" but "your hook is one line, your rehook is the second line, and together they must form a complete thought within LinkedIn's three-line preview." Not "be credible" but "insert a signposting phrase that makes your advice untransferable." This level of tactical specificity is what separates actionable content advice from motivational noise. Every element maps to a measurable platform behavior: dwell time, scroll patterns, comment depth.
