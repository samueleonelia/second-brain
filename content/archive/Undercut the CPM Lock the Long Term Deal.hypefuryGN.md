# Undercut the CPM Lock the Long Term Deal

## The Story

By the end of 2023 Mindstream was selling sponsorships against ~150,000 subscribers. The Rundown, Superhuman, and AI Tool Report (the competitors Adam tracked weekly) were charging at the high end of the AI-newsletter market. Adam priced Mindstream's ads beneath them, and the logic he applied was the opposite of revenue maximization.

_"we would just undercut slightly"_

_"we kind of kept our rates a little bit lower than industry standard"_

The pricing decision compounded with two other choices that made it work. First, Mindstream measured actual advertiser ROI honestly and shared the numbers, so the lower CPM coincided with better-than-benchmark results, which is the combination that produces renewals instead of one-shots. Second, Adam refused to raise prices every time the list grew by 10,000 subscribers, instead locking advertisers who worked into multi-month deals at the rate they signed at. Their results improved over time without their cost going up.

The retention math was the point.

_"we had more than 50% retention with our advertisers"_

_"the way we kept long-term customers is pricing at a position that just made so much sense for them"_

Over 50% advertiser retention is high enough that Adam could project advertising revenue like a recurring number instead of starting from zero every month. Sam Parr's stories about The Hustle had taught him the opposite scenario: have a great month, then go win the same deals again next month. The pricing-undercut posture also let him operate without a sales team or a customer success hire. The labor cost of replacing churning advertisers was higher than the foregone revenue from keeping CPMs honest.

_"my rationale was let's get kind of a little bit less revenue than we could but then not have to hire a sales person and a customer success people and churn through advertisers at like a a quick rate"_

## Lesson for Creators

The highest CPM you can extract is rarely the most profitable price. When your alternative is hiring a sales team to refill the funnel every month, underpricing the inventory and locking the relationship is the move that keeps you operating on the founder's calendar instead of the salesperson's. Advertiser retention above 50% is what lets a newsletter forecast like a SaaS product.
