# Coaching Equals Half the Revenue

## The Story

Olivia Wickstrom does not stop at the subscribe button. _"half of my monthly income comes from Substack paid subscriptions"_, she writes, and the other half comes from coaching the writers her publication attracts. The two are one pipeline, not two businesses.

It runs in stages. A reader meets her in the Notes feed and subscribes. Free essays build trust over weeks. At some point they catch a live coaching session and see how she works in real time. Eventually they upgrade to paid, and when they are ready, they book a 1:1 call. Each layer warms the buyer for the next, higher-ticket one, which is why the coaching revenue grows as fast as the subscriptions.

## Lesson for Creators

Paid subscriptions are a floor, not a ceiling. Build the ladder, free to subscriber to live to high-ticket offer, so the trust you already earned funds the work that pays the most.
