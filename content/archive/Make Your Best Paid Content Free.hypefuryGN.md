# Make Your Best Paid Content Free

## The Story

Olivia Wickstrom's counterintuitive monetization move is to give away the posts she could most easily sell. Once a month she takes a tactical deep dive that would normally live behind the paywall and opens it to everyone. _"Free content is what drives paid subscriptions"_, she writes, and her three most viral posts, on Notes, on Pinterest, and on going full-time, each drove thousands of free subscribers.

The math is the whole argument. Paywalled, one of those posts might have earned twenty new paid subscribers. Free, it brought thousands of readers who convert to paid over the following months, after a free flagship shows them exactly how good the paid material is.

## Lesson for Creators

Your best free post is your best sales asset. Give away one flagship a month, because the readers it converts over time outnumber the handful a paywall would have captured.
