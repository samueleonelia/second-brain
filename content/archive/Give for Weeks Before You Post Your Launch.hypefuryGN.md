# Give for Weeks Before You Post Your Launch

## The Story

Maja's go-to channel for the first few hundred customers is other people's communities, but she is strict about how you enter one. Walking in cold to announce a launch reads as spam: you are an intruder and nobody celebrates your arrival.

Her rule is "always give before you get." The warm-up is concrete: "if you just like spend a couple of weeks before and just like give a couple of useful information," through value comments and value posts, your eventual launch post gets accepted instead of resented.

The strongest version adds the gatekeeper. If you can earn the active admin's blessing before you post, that endorsement is something no one in the community will argue with, and it converts borrowed attention into welcome attention.

## Lesson for Creators

A borrowed community is earned, not raided. Contribute real value for weeks and win the admin's endorsement before you ever post your launch, so the audience receives you as a member rather than an intruder.
