# Geoff Sharpe

Geoff Sharpe is the founder of Lookout Media, a bootstrapped network of local-news newsletters in Canada — the Ottawa Lookout, Van City Lookout, Capital Eats, and Yukon Lookout. Unlike most local-newsletter operators, who run free, ad-supported event-aggregation products, Lookout monetizes primarily through paid memberships (roughly 75% of revenue) built on original hard journalism. By 2025 it was approaching a $500K annual run rate with about 3,000 paying members and a free list near 100,000, grown almost entirely through paid Meta ads. Geoff is not a journalist; he funds his living through a separate marketing agency and reinvests Lookout's profit into hiring reporters.

- [[Run a Membership Drive Like a Pledge Campaign]] -- concentrate ~25% of annual revenue into one time-boxed December drive built as a mission-plus-deadline story arc
- [[Buy the First Year of Growth in Month Three]] -- fund list growth with paid Meta ads, treating acquisition as a timeline problem you can pay to compress
- [[Optimize Ads for Who Converts, Not Who's Cheapest]] -- buy against member LTV, not cheapest cost-per-lead; pay more for news leads that convert
- [[Prove the Model by Relaunching It in a Harder Market]] -- relaunch in competitive Vancouver and benchmark month-for-month against Ottawa to prove the playbook travels
- [[Don't Build What Your Audience Says They Want]] -- keep the paid hard-news core even though events get the most clicks, because stated demand isn't willingness-to-pay
- [[Pay the Next Hire Before You Pay Yourself]] -- take zero founder salary and reinvest every dollar of profit into another journalist, never running a deficit
- [[Make the Writer the Thing Readers Trust]] -- put a named, non-anonymous writer front and center and convert that trust into sold-out paid events
- [[Spend on Retention Because Growth Is the Easy Part]] -- automate growth with ads and pour scarce attention into onboarding, segmentation, and churn reduction
