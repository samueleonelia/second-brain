# One Post, Many Pins

## The Story

Olivia Wickstrom does not write new material for Pinterest. She stretches what she already published. _"Every post becomes multiple pins"_, she explains, with one essay yielding a title-graphic pin, a pin built on a single tip from it, a lifestyle photo, and a quote graphic, each a different doorway into the same piece.

The work stays light because she batches it: open Canva, pull up a few templates, swap in new titles and images, and produce a week of pins in one sitting. She is not reinventing the idea, she is reframing it enough times that Pinterest gets many chances to match it to someone's search.

## Lesson for Creators

One post is raw material for a dozen assets. Atomize each piece into several angles and batch them against templates, so a single idea works many search queries instead of one.
