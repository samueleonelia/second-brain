# The Digicel Fortune 500 Turning Point

## The Story

Digicel, a Caribbean telecommunications operator, reached out to Jasmin through Upwork to develop messaging across 30 markets (Source 1). It was a Fortune 500 client, the kind of opportunity he had never had.

He initially declined. Self-doubt told him he wasn't ready for that level of work (Source 1).

Then they reached out again. His friend Jack encouraged him to accept. Jasmin took the call (Source 1).

In that first conversation, he generated approximately 20 strategic ideas. The final deliverable was a two-word universal messaging strategy that would work across all 30 markets (Source 1).

The client's feedback reframed his entire career: "I'm not hiring you for your copywriting... I'm hiring you for your ideas and because of your mind" (Source 1).

That single moment shifted Jasmin from seeing himself as a copywriter to understanding he was a strategist. The Digicel project remains active and is one of his longest-running client relationships. He describes it as "one of the most interesting projects" he's ever worked on (Source 1).

The shift also changed his business model. "The key difference: freelancing clients provide guidance; businesses set the guidance." He went from being hired for execution to being hired for direction (Source 1).

## Lesson for Creators

Jasmin almost said no to the project that redefined his career. Self-doubt nearly cost him a Fortune 500 client and the identity shift from copywriter to strategist. The client's feedback, "I'm not hiring you for your copywriting," is the kind of external signal that changes how you price, position, and describe yourself. Sometimes it takes a client to see what you can't see in yourself. And sometimes it takes a friend to push you to answer the phone.
