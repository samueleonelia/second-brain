# Editing the Link in After the First Hour

## The Story

A link in a LinkedIn post cuts its reach hard, down to about a quarter of what it would have gotten clean. That collides with Tom Alder's other rule, that the first hour decides everything. So he separates the two jobs in time. For the first hour the post carries no link at all and is optimized purely for engagement, activity, and conversation. Once that critical window has done its work, he edits the post and adds the newsletter CTA at the bottom.

Editing in a link does dent reach, but by then the first-hour signal that drives the post has already been banked. He pairs this with a second move: dropping a comment on his own post after the ten-minute mark (never first, since being first to comment is also penalized) where the newsletter can be plugged.

_"you want to edit the post after 1 hour, so you leave the post without any link for the first hour, optimized for engagement"_

## Lesson for Creators

Do not make the algorithm choose between your reach and your conversion. Win the first hour with a clean post, then edit the call-to-action in once the reach is locked.
