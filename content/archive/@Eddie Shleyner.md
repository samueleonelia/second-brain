# Eddie Shleyner

Eddie Shleyner is the founder of VeryGoodCopy, a newsletter about copywriting and creativity built on short "micro-lessons" (a copywriting principle wrapped in a story, sized to fit a LinkedIn post). A former Copy Chief at G2, he grew VeryGoodCopy from a private Google Doc of notes he wrote while teaching himself copywriting into a large owned audience: his own site cites growth of roughly 100 newsletter subscribers a day, Growth In Reverse put the list near 68,000 subscribers in December 2024, and his LinkedIn following runs in the range of 110,000 to 128,000. He is the author of *Very Good Copy: 207 Micro-Lessons on Thinking and Writing Like a Copywriter* (2024). His growth is a LinkedIn-plus-owned-newsletter story, not an Instagram one.

- [[Micro-Lessons Sized to the LinkedIn Feed]] -- engineered each lesson to LinkedIn's ~220-word, 1,300-character limit so it posts natively with no reformatting
- [[Documenting the Job He Didn't Know Yet]] -- built the content library by documenting what he learned while teaching himself copywriting on the job
- [[Borrowing HubSpot's Audience to Seed His Own]] -- pitched onto HubSpot's blog with no audience and used an in-post backlink to seed his own list
- [[The Newsletter-Powered LinkedIn Growth Loop]] -- used the owned newsletter to amplify his LinkedIn posts and convert the new reach back into subscribers
- [[A Website Built to Do One Thing]] -- pointed every page and article at a single conversion goal, newsletter signup
