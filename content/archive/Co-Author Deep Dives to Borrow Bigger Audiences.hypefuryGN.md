# Co-Author Deep Dives to Borrow Bigger Audiences

## The Story

Building the GTM Strategist newsletter, Maja did not try to out-publish bigger names. She partnered with them. In 2024 she ran co-authored deep dives and podcast placements with creators whose audiences dwarfed hers: Sean Ellis, Kyle Poyar of Growth Unhinged, Aakash Gupta, and Pawel Huryn.

The collaboration with Kyle Poyar, a piece on building a GTM strategy from scratch published to his list, became one of her best-performing pieces ever. As she wrote, never had she imagined that "one of those articles would be the most popular on the Substack" she herself reads daily.

The arbitrage only works if the borrowed audience gets something worth their attention. Her rule was quality, not a quick swap of promo blurbs: "my team and I put in at least 30 hours of very hard work" on each shared asset, with hands-on examples rather than ChatGPT filler. The effort is what earned the introduction to someone else's trust.

## Lesson for Creators

Borrow reach instead of grinding for it. Co-create genuinely useful work with creators whose audience is bigger than yours, and over-invest in the quality of the shared asset so their readers convert into yours.
