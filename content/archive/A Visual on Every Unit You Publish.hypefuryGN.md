# A Visual on Every Unit You Publish

## The Story

Pueyo's distribution is visual by default. Per Growth In Reverse, every tweet in his threads carries a picture or a data visualization, sometimes an auto-playing animated map, which slows the scroll and pulls the eye in. His articles run dense with visuals, on the order of one image per 130 to 140 words.

The maps and charts are not decoration; they are the hook and the proof at once, which is why his threads spread. One February 2023 thread drew well over 100,000 likes. The format itself, not just the argument, is the differentiator: in a feed full of text, the piece that is built from visuals is the one that gets seen and screenshotted.

## Lesson for Creators

In a feed, a wall of text loses. Pair every post, every tweet in a thread, and every section with a visual that carries the point on its own. The visual is what stops the scroll and what gets screenshotted and shared.
