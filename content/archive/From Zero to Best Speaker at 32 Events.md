# From Zero to Best Speaker at 32 Events

## The Story

Jasmin Alic has spoken at 40+ events across 20+ countries (Source 2). He has been voted "Best Speaker" at 32 of those events (Source 1).

His 2025 speaking schedule alone includes 19 events: University of Maryland (USA), infestival in Bratislava, LinkedIn Influencer Summit, Dan-D Social Media Day in Ljubljana, Linkiaden in Oslo, Socialize in Skopje, Linked Summit in Odense, I-Con in Limassol, a USA LinkedIn Tour across Chicago, New York, and San Francisco, Split Tech City Festival, DMM Conference in Zagreb, MBF in Rovinj, a conference in Athens, MYB Summit in Marbella, IT Reboot Festival in Travnik, WITCON in Skopje, and LiDay in Prague (Source 1).

He spoke at TEDx Sarajevo In Focus. A testimonial from that event: "Jasmin changed the way I think about every word online" (Source 1).

Other testimonials: "What Jasmin condenses in just 30 minutes surpasses hours of other expert courses. Time with Jasmin is invaluable" (infestival, Slovakia) (Source 1). "His session alone justified the ticket price" (iCON, Cyprus) (Source 1). "Squeezed a 1-hour copywriting masterclass into 30 minutes" (AllWeb Conference, Albania) (Source 1). "His presentation was perfect as the final talk and the best ever seen" (Linked Summit, Denmark) (Source 1).

He was featured in Forbes, Bloomberg, Financial Times, and HubSpot (Source 3). He was named in the Top 100 Most Influential Men of 2025 by Leadership Up Magazine and received the Man of the Year Award 2025 from Dama and Dzentlmen Godine (Source 2).

## Lesson for Creators

Jasmin's speaking career was built entirely on the back of his LinkedIn presence. No speaking agent, no traditional media career. He posted consistently, became the #1 creator on the platform, and conference organizers came to him. The testimonials reveal his edge: density. He delivers a masterclass worth of value in a 30-minute slot. For creators considering speaking, the lesson is that your platform audience is your audition tape. Conference organizers are watching.
