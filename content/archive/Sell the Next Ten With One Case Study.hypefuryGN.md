# Sell the Next Ten With One Case Study

## The Story

On a complex knowledge-graph AI agent for supply-chain traceability, Maja did not start with a broad campaign. From business development at events they found one champion: an early adopter who believed in the technology and wanted to try it.

They built the architecture and a working prototype for that one customer's use case, measured the results, and turned the win into an asset. "we constructed a case study and then we presented it with that specific client on webinars on industry events," with a little PR layered on top. The sale was the case study.

Then they cloned the motion. "you helped one person now how can we find five or 10 different people that need the same thing." One proven deployment became the proof that unlocked a cluster of near-identical buyers.

## Lesson for Creators

In a hard market, win one believer completely before chasing many. Co-build a real result with a single champion, package it as a case study, and use that one proof to sell the next ten customers who share the same problem.
