# The Nine-Month Daily Posting Streak

## The Story

The 35,000-subscriber newsletter did not start with the newsletter. It started two and a half years earlier with a private dare. Tom Alder set himself a challenge to post every single day for 30 days just to see if he could do it. He kept going. The 30 days became nine months of unbroken daily posting, weekends included, and the account climbed to 10,000 followers.

Growth was slow at first and he was not yet trying to build a business. The streak was the asset. When he later returned with a deliberate plan to spin a business out of the audience, he was not starting from zero, he was restarting from a base of roughly 12,000 to 13,000 followers that the streak had built.

_"ended up doing it for for nine months straight posting every single day including weekend and and got to 10,000 followers"_

## Lesson for Creators

The audience that makes a launch easy is built in the boring months nobody watches. A fixed daily-posting commitment, held long past the point where it feels productive, is what produces the follower base you later convert.
