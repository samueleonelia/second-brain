# The First Hour Decides the Post

## The Story

Tom Alder can roughly predict a post's final reach from its first hour. Around 100 reactions in the opening hour tends to land near 500 over the post's 48-to-72-hour life. Every post he counts as viral, the ones that reached 1,000 or 2,000 reactions, cleared 200-plus reactions inside that first hour.

So he refuses to post and walk away. During that opening window he is there replying to every comment, and replying in a way designed to pull more conversation out of the original commenter or anyone watching the thread. The activity in the first hour is the lever, and he pulls it manually.

_"the first hour right after you post is by far the most important time period in determining the success of the post"_

## Lesson for Creators

Reach is decided before lunch. Block the hour after you publish for live replies, treat early reactions as a leading indicator, and judge whether a post is working by its first sixty minutes rather than its first day.
