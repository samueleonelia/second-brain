# Research Trending Topics With the Last 30 Days Skill

## The Story

The first stage of Duncan Rogoff's content pipeline is research, and it answers the question most creators skip: what does my audience actually want to hear about right now? He runs a Claude Code skill he calls "last 30 days." _"it basically researches the last 30 days of trending topic across Reddit, X, Blue Sky, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Happy News, Polymarket, and the web, and it finds what the community is actually upvoting and talking about."_

The output isn't a news summary. It's a demand signal. _"you basically have your entire audience telling you exactly what they want to hear from you."_ He weights conversation platforms like Reddit and X higher than headlines on purpose, because the goal is the argument behind the news, not the news itself: _"you don't want to just be creating content on the news, you want to be creating content on what people are really discussing."_

That distinction is the whole point. Posting about an event gets you parity with everyone else posting the same event. Posting about what people are arguing about underneath it gets you relevance.

The research isn't the end of the workflow. It's the targeting system for everything after it. Duncan reads the output, picks the single thread his audience is most engaged with, and hands that exact topic to the next skill. In the video he points at one trending thread and tells the system: _"I want you to do some further research on this and then create a relevant lead magnet for a high-level audience."_ The lead magnet and the posts that follow are all aimed at a topic he already knows people want.

## Lesson for Creators

Start every piece from demand, not from a blank page. Before writing, pull what your audience is actively upvoting and debating across the platforms where they actually talk, then write into that conversation. The research step is what turns a hopeful guess into something you already know will land.
