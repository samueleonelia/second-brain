# Launching to 5,000 Subscribers Before the First Edition

## The Story

Tom Alder did not wait for a finished product to start collecting subscribers. He was already posting on LinkedIn every day, so he simply bolted a call-to-action onto the bottom of that existing content: if you like this, I am launching a newsletter soon, go subscribe. The link pointed to a near-blank landing page, the first version little more than a mailing list with a coming-soon line. A week later he added a short welcome email that admitted he did not yet know exactly what the publication would become.

The point was that the audience, the impressions, and the traffic already existed. The landing page just gave that attention somewhere to convert. Over roughly a two-month window, with a couple of posts that blew up and pushed a few hundred subscribers each, the list filled up on its own.

_"by the time my first email went out I was yeah ballpark 5,000 maybe four and a half thousand subscribers"_

## Lesson for Creators

You do not need the product before you start the list. If you already have any audience, attach a subscribe CTA to the content you are posting anyway and send it to a placeholder page. The first edition can go out to thousands instead of to friends and family.
