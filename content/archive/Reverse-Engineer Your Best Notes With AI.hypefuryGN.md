# Reverse-Engineer Your Best Notes With AI

## The Story

Most people post a Note, hope, and move on. Olivia Wickstrom closes the loop. Once a week she opens her Substack Growth tab, finds the Notes that brought in the most subscribers and revenue, then pastes those winners into Claude or ChatGPT and asks it to name what they have in common, topic, tone, format, length, hooks, and to generate ten new ideas built on the same patterns.

_"You're essentially reverse-engineering your own success"_, she writes, and the point is that an outside model catches the patterns she is too close to see. The data already exists in the dashboard; the AI just turns it into the next ten posts.

## Lesson for Creators

Your own analytics are a content brief you are probably ignoring. Feed your top performers to an AI, ask it what they share, and let the answer write your next batch instead of guessing from scratch.
