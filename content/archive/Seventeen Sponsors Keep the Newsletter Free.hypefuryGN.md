# Seventeen Sponsors Keep the Newsletter Free

## The Story

Maja keeps the GTM Strategist Substack and LinkedIn newsletter free to every reader, and pays for it with sponsors rather than a paywall. "In 2024, 17 partners supported my content," with the biggest backers being Clay, Amplitude, HubSpot, and MeetRecord.

The economics are explicit to her readers. Sponsor money is what removes the subscription ask: "Your support helps keep this Substack free for everyone." It also funds the team and the time that goes into the content.

Keeping the front door free is what makes the sponsorships worth buying. A larger ungated audience across the Substack and the 52K-follower LinkedIn presence is the inventory she sells, so free reach and sponsor revenue reinforce each other instead of competing.

## Lesson for Creators

Sponsorship and free reach compound. Keep the newsletter open to everyone, sell the resulting audience to a roster of flat-fee brand partners, and let sponsor revenue, not reader paywalls, fund the work.
