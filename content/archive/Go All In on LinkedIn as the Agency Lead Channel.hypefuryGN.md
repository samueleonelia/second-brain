# Go All In on LinkedIn as the Agency Lead Channel

## The Story

Duncan Rogoff spent over a decade as a motion designer and senior art director for brands like Apple, PlayStation, and Nissan before reinventing himself as an AI-automation creator. When he decided to grow an audience, he did not spread himself thin across every platform. He made one deliberate bet: LinkedIn. _"A few months ago, I made a decision to go all in on building my brand on LinkedIn, knowing that the professional connections I would make would likely lead to a lot of leads for my business."_

The logic was channel-fit, not raw reach. His offer is an AI-automation agency, and the people who buy that are professionals and operators, which is exactly who is on LinkedIn. He puts the choice bluntly for anyone selling B2B: _"What is the best social media platform to publish your content on if you want to actually book automation clients? And I'll give you a hint, it's not YouTube."_

The bet paid out on two scoreboards at once. On audience, _"in just a couple of months, I went from 1,500 followers to over 10,000 followers."_ On revenue, _"LinkedIn is one of the main platforms I've used to grow my AI agency to over $10,000 a month and sell my automations to thousands of customers."_ He uses YouTube as the teaching channel and LinkedIn as the lead channel, and never confuses the role of one for the other.

## Lesson for Creators

Pick the platform where your buyer already is, not the one with the largest audience. For a B2B offer, a focused LinkedIn presence can out-earn a much larger following on a platform where the buyers never transact. Duncan measured the bet by inbound leads, not vanity reach, which is why he could tell within months that the concentration was working.
