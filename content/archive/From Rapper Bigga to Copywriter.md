# From Rapper Bigga to Copywriter

## The Story

Jasmin Alic spent five years as a hip-hop artist performing under the name "Bigga," part of the rap group MouseMiles with Vedad Mesanovic (Source 4). During his music career, he wrote every one of his songs, did his own PR and media outreach, handled marketing and social media, and wrote his website (Source 1).

In 2014, he and his best friend applied to X-Factor, passed bootcamp, delivered in the Judge's Houses round, and were offered a studio contract before the Finals. They quit on their own just before the Finals (Source 3). He was also featured on MTV (Source 4). By 2015, he quit music entirely (Source 1).

On his first day on Upwork, he saw the word "copywriting." "No idea what it means. I google it. It's ALL the stuff I'd been doing for years!" (Source 1). He realized that the promotional writing, the PR, the social media content -- everything he'd done as a rapper was copywriting. The transition felt less like a career change and more like a relabeling (Source 3).

The LinkedIn post where he shared this origin story received 960 likes and 634 comments (Source 1).

## Lesson for Creators

Career pivots don't always mean starting over. Jasmin's five years writing rap lyrics, managing his own PR, and running social media gave him every skill a copywriter needs. He just didn't know the industry term for it. The lesson: your previous career may have already trained you for your next one. The gap is often just vocabulary, not competence.
