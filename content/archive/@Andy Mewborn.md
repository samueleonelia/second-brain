# Andy Mewborn

Andy Mewborn is the founder and CEO of Distribute (distribute.so), an all-in-one workspace for go-to-market teams. An early product-and-sales hire at Outreach, he spent seven years there before co-founding the LinkedIn tool Taplio and selling it to lempire. He was early to LinkedIn content and built a large personal following there, funneling it into a newsletter he pegs at 31,000-plus subscribers, and he productized his own playbook as Brand 30, a 30-day personal-brand challenge (later the go-to-market Elite Circle). His growth thesis: treat content like performance marketing, write for the buyer rather than the feed, and let brand and community be the only durable moats.
