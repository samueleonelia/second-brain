# Katelyn Bourgoin

Katelyn Bourgoin is a buyer-psychology expert and the founder of *Unignorable* (be unignorable), the ownable-ideas agency. After a VC-backed startup went bankrupt, she started the *Why We Buy* newsletter as a pipeline builder for a customer-research agency; the newsletter took off instead, growing past 60,000 email subscribers (Kit case study) and roughly 220,000 across platforms (*Growth In Reverse*). She built a following on X from 2020, then grew to roughly 92,000 LinkedIn followers, and now teaches expert entrepreneurs to find the one idea they can be known for.

- [[Newsletter as Pipeline Bait, Then Follow the Signal]] -- launched a newsletter as lead-gen for another business, then pivoted the company into the topic the audience actually rewarded
- [[Bootstrap a New Platform With Content That Already Won]] -- entered LinkedIn by republishing the X threads that had already proven themselves, skipping the cold-start guessing phase
- [[The Authority Flywheel Around One Ownable Idea]] -- repeats one ownable idea across four rotating angles (problem, POV, promise, proof) so repetition compounds authority
- [[Research as the Moat Instead of Algorithm Hacks]] -- grew without comment pods or trend-chasing by pouring buyer-psychology research into the content itself
- [[A 52-Page Voice Doc That Lets AI Draft in Your Voice]] -- encoded her strategy, voice, and frameworks into a book-length AI co-writer that drafts on-brand in half the time
