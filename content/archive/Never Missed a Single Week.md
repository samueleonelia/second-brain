# Never Missed a Single Week

## The Story

Asked for his single most important piece of advice, Matt's answer is the cadence rule: "I have not missed a single week of VulnerableU since I started it. Consistency is like the number one thing I tell everybody" (Source 1).

The cadence is mechanical: newsletter goes out every Friday morning, written Thursday night. Readers know exactly when to expect it (Source 1).

There is one acceptable exit, and it doesn't include skipping: "If you're going to have a break, announce it. Get a guest writer for those two weeks. Do not miss the time" (Source 1).

He frames the same discipline in a separate interview, attributing the success of the project to it directly: "Daniel and I have both been writing weekly newsletters for years. Consistency is the real growth engine" (Source 2). And he names the part of the work the creator can control: "Some videos flop. Some take off. You can't predict it. But you can control whether you keep posting" (Source 2).

## Lesson for Creators

The thing most creators optimize for — quality of any individual issue — is largely outside their control once a baseline is hit. What is fully inside their control is whether the issue shipped at all, on the day readers expect it. Matt treats showing up as the load-bearing variable: viral hits are luck on top of the cadence, never a substitute for it. The "skip a week" decision is the one to refuse hardest, because the rule that breaks isn't "this one issue" — it's "you can trust this to arrive Friday." Rebuild that trust slower than you broke it.
