# The Pat Walls Content Swap That Got 5-10K Subscribers

## The Story

In June 2023, Alex Garcia published a thread and article about how Pat Walls of Starter Story grew his email list (Source 1). A month later, in July 2023, Pat Walls published an interview with Alex on his YouTube channel. The video got over 80,000 views (Source 1).

Garcia says this "swap" got him an additional 5,000 to 10,000 subscribers (Source 1). For context, a "typical" newsletter swap might get a few hundred subscribers. This collaboration was "so unique, and ended up benefiting both sides really well" (Source 1).

The mechanic was simple: Garcia gave Pat free, high-quality exposure to his audience. Pat reciprocated with a long-form video interview to his audience. Both formats (written breakdown and video interview) played to each creator's strengths and platform (Source 1).

## Lesson for Creators

The swap worked because it wasn't a swap in the traditional sense (cross-promotion emails). It was two creators making each other the subject of their best content. Garcia wrote a genuine analysis of Pat's growth. Pat made a genuine interview with Garcia. The audience on each side got value, not a promo. That's why the conversion rate was 10-50x higher than typical newsletter swaps. The lesson: don't trade ads. Trade content.
