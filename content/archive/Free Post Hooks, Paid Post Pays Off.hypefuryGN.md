# Free Post Hooks, Paid Post Pays Off

## The Story

Pueyo publishes on a paired rhythm: a free post and a paid post. Per Growth In Reverse, the free post is built to be shareable and ends on a high-curiosity hook, for example teasing an end-of-the-world-in-15-years claim, and the paid follow-up answers the question a day or two later. A free post, *When Will AI Take Your Job*, set up a paid one, *How Fast Will AI Automation Arrive?* The free post does the reach; the open loop does the converting.

He reinforces it with quarterly recap posts that link a mostly paywalled archive and tease where the ideas are heading, pulling free readers back toward the paywall on a schedule. Free and paid are not two products here; they are two halves of one funnel.

## Lesson for Creators

Do not treat free and paid as separate products. Use the free post as the hook and the paid post as the payoff, with a deliberate open loop between them. Reach and revenue stop competing the moment the thing that travels is also the thing that sells the upgrade.
