# £12,500 a Year: The Founder Salary That Built a Six-Figure Business

## The Story

Milly Tamati pays herself £12,500 a year while running a six-figure business (Source 1). She reinvests most of the revenue into improving the member experience (Source 1).

Generalist World revenue: $50,000 in 2023, $63,300 in 2024 (Source 2). The team is lean: two full-time employees (Milly and community manager Ece) plus a few contractors (Source 1). Milly's first hire was Ece, who excels in execution and operational excellence (Source 4).

The business is bootstrapped with no venture capital (Source 3). Milly deliberately avoided VC funding and resists the urge to scale aggressively (Source 3). She prioritizes maintaining a calm lifestyle on the Isle of Raasay: forest walks when the weather permits, cooking, playing guitar (Source 3, Source 4).

She describes the team as feeling "like family" and emphasizes shared values in long-term collaboration (Source 4). She acknowledges that operational details and processes aren't her strongest areas, which is why Ece's hire was critical (Source 4).

## Lesson for Creators

£12,500 is below minimum wage in most Western countries. Milly's choice to pay herself that little isn't a failure. It's a capital allocation strategy. Every pound reinvested into the community increases the value for the 600+ paying members, which sustains growth and attracts sponsorships. The business is profitable but deliberately small. The island lifestyle isn't a compromise. It's the design. She's building a business around a life, not a life around a business.
