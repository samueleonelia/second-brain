# The 30 Percent Equity List Acquisition

## The Story

Five months after launch, Mindstream sat at 15,000 subscribers and was burning the obvious paid-growth dollars on Beehiiv Boosts and LinkedIn ads. Adam Biddlecombe was networking with another newsletter operator, Matitz, a Slovenian developer who had accidentally collected ~200,000 emails on his domain autogpt.net. The open-source AutoGPT project blew up in mid-2023, every search for `autogpt` landed on Matitz's SEO-optimized page, and a join-now button silently filled his list. He had the audience and no idea how to run a publication. Adam had the publication and no audience.

The pitch Adam made was structural, not transactional. Matitz would not sell the list. He would receive 30 percent equity in Mindstream and merge his subscribers, his SEO traffic, and his existing product revenue into the company. He would come on board as CTO. The deal closed and Mindstream did the part that mattered most: it did not import the whole 200K. It imported only the engaged subscribers, landing at 110,000 overnight.

_"I managed to broker a deal that he would get 30% equity in Mindream and then he would bring all of his um newsletter subscribers"_

_"We didn't import the whole list, we imported the most active people"_

The math justified the dilution. Buying the same 100,000 quality subscribers through paid cross-promotion at the going rate would have cost roughly $150,000 in cash, and Mindstream did not have $150,000. Selling 30 percent of an early-stage newsletter was the cheaper currency, and unlike paid acquisition the trade also brought a working co-founder who plugged the SEO-traffic flywheel back into the company.

_"if we were to go out and and uh get 100,000 subscribers at that sort of quality through like paid strategy, we'd probably be paying best case uh$150 per subscriber"_

That single transaction is what flipped the business from scrappy to fundable. Within four months of crossing 100K, HubSpot was advertising. Two months after that, HubSpot was in acquisition talks.

## Lesson for Creators

Equity is currency too, and at the early stage it is usually cheaper than cash. A founder with an accidental list is often a better trade partner than a paid-acquisition channel, because the equity binds them into the operation instead of leaving them as a one-time growth spike. The discipline is in the import filter: do not take the dead weight, only the engaged subs that will keep your open rate honest for the advertisers you have not signed yet.
