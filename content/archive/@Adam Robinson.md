# Adam Robinson

Adam Robinson is the bootstrapped founder and CEO of Retention.com and RB2B, two B2B SaaS companies with combined annual revenue around $30M, both grown with tiny teams and no venture capital. Starting on LinkedIn in January 2022, he turned a personal profile into his companies' primary customer-acquisition channel, building past 120,000 LinkedIn followers by treating founder-led content as a deliberate craft. He is the clearest working example of "inbound-led outbound": using an owned audience, a free product, and a manufactured-but-authentic public persona to make organic social the entire go-to-market.

- [[Content Market Fit Before Any Demo]] -- posts daily for ~9-12 months judged on recognition, not demos, until a repeatable voice emerges
- [[Saying One Thing a Thousand Ways]] -- pick 2-3 authentic, polarizing content pillars and repeat each endlessly in fresh forms
- [[Every Post Assumes Nobody Knows You]] -- write the hook first and front-load credibility every post, because post-to-post audience overlap is ~15%
- [[The Ten-Person Commenting Play]] -- comment thoughtfully on ~10 influential people for months to earn reciprocal and second/third-degree reach
- [[Widening the Attack Surface]] -- get co-founder and employees posting too, recruiting them by selling posting as a portable, career-making skill
- [[Inbound-Led Outbound Through the Profile]] -- the audience makes outbound book at near-100% even when a teammate takes the call
- [[The Free Offer as a Megaphone]] -- pair a big audience with a genuinely free product so the audience turns a free offer into word-of-mouth virality
