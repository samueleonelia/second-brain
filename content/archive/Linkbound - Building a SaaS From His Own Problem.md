# Linkbound: Building a SaaS From His Own Problem

## The Story

Jasmin Alic co-founded Linkbound with Senad Šantić, CEO of ZenDev, a Swedish development agency (Source 1). It's a Chrome extension for warm outreach on LinkedIn: it filters and identifies people who have already engaged with your posts through likes, comments, or shares (Source 1).

The tool hit $25,000 ARR within its first week of launch. 500 users installed the Chrome extension over a single weekend (Source 1).

Performance numbers: 93% response rate to messages sent to qualified leads, compared to 1-3% for cold outreach. 87% of those responses resulted in booked meetings (Source 1).

Features include engagement analytics, advanced filtering by company, size, industry, location, and position, custom dynamic lists that auto-update, CRM-like interaction tracking, and AI-powered personalized messaging recommendations (Source 1).

Senad Šantić on the product philosophy: "The biggest lesson we learned is that if you're solving your problem, the first level of product-market fit should be attained when you, the creators, use the product" (Source 1).

Linkbound became Jasmin's third business alongside Hey Jay (brand coaching) and Link Up (educational community) (Source 2, Source 3).

## Lesson for Creators

Jasmin built Linkbound from the problem he faced daily: thousands of people engaging with his LinkedIn posts but no efficient way to convert that engagement into business conversations. The 93% response rate versus 1-3% for cold outreach captures why warm outreach works: you're reaching people who already raised their hand. For creators with large audiences, the gap between "people who engage with my content" and "people I can talk to" is where a product can live.
