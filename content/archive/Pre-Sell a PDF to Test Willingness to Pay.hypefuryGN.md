# Pre-Sell a PDF to Test Willingness to Pay

## The Story

Asked how to measure willingness to pay, Maja rejects the survey. Stated preferences lie, and a questionnaire about price tells you almost nothing about behavior.

Her test is a transaction. "create PDF create like a PowerPoint presentation and try to sell it try to pre-sell it people are like shut up and take my money." If people open their wallets for a deck, you have signal; if they hesitate, you learn that too, before building anything.

The pre-sale does double duty: it validates and it funds. Collecting pre-orders or pre-commitments keeps the lights on and co-finances development. Her stance is blunt: "I'm anti- survey but I'm definitely into early monetization."

## Lesson for Creators

The only reliable willingness-to-pay test is a charge. Package the idea as a PDF or deck, try to pre-sell it, and let real payments, not survey answers, decide whether to build.
