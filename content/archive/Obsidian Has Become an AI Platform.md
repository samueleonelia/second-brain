# Obsidian Has Become an AI Platform

An X article by Khairallah AL-Awady, "30 Obsidian Workflows, Plugins, and Setups That Most Users Don't Know," frames Obsidian's quiet shift from note app to AI platform. Per the article preview: Obsidian has over 2,700 community plugins, more than 100 of which are AI-related, and the CEO of Obsidian personally published official Claude Skills for the platform, which collected over 12,900 GitHub stars.

The signal is the official endorsement. AI integration in Obsidian is no longer a community hack layered on top; the platform's own leadership ships first-party tooling for AI agents to work inside vaults. For a local-files, markdown-based tool, this is a natural fit: plain text files are the interface agents already speak best.
