# Repackage Each Piece as a Viral Twitter Thread

## The Story

The reach that built Pueyo did not come from his website or his newsletter. It came from Twitter threads. Per Growth In Reverse, his single biggest growth lever is repackaging each deep-research piece into a thread crafted to travel: one cohesive story, an opening tweet that works as a curiosity hook (often an auto-playing map or chart that makes you wonder what it shows), and a visualization or picture in every single tweet so a scrolling reader keeps slowing down to read the next one.

The numbers track the channel, not the essay alone. Before the Coronavirus threads in March 2020 he had about 450 Twitter followers; by April he had over 35,000, more than a thousand new followers a day. It was not a one-time event. In February 2023 a single thread (124,000+ likes, roughly 30,000 retweets) took him from 101,000 to over 170,000 followers in a week. He is not even a consistent poster, taking weeks off and then returning with another viral thread, because what travels is the formula, not the cadence.

## Lesson for Creators

Deep research is invisible until it is packaged for the channel that actually distributes. Pueyo's move is to convert each long piece into a thread built to travel: one story, a hook that stops the scroll, and an image on every tweet. The depth earns the trust; the thread does the reach.
