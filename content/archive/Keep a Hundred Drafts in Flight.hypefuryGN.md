# Keep a Hundred Drafts in Flight

## The Story

Pueyo does not write from a blank page each week. Per Growth In Reverse he keeps 100 to 150 drafts in progress at once, collecting notes on a topic for months or years before it is ready. He also works in repeatable series, like his geo-history of one country at a time across Mexico, Jamaica, Hungary, and Chile.

On Summation he points to the next one in the queue. He has not published Argentina yet, but as he puts it, "I already have the draft," and he already knows "a lot of what to say about it." The standing backlog plus the series template means he is never starting cold. He is always finishing something that has been maturing for a while.

## Lesson for Creators

Consistency is not willpower; it is inventory. Keep far more drafts alive than you publish, and work in repeatable formats, so every week you ship the strongest ready piece instead of inventing one under deadline.
