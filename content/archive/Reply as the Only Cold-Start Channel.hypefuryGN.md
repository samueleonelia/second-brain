# Reply as the Only Cold-Start Channel

## The Story

When Erica Schneider started building an audience in 2022, she made the mistake she now warns every beginner about: she assumed her title would carry her. As a head of content, she figured people would see the badge on her profile, read what she said about writing, and follow. They did not. On The Bootstrapped Founder she was blunt about it: those random people will listen "like absolutely not."

Her fix was to stop broadcasting and start replying. Posting into a feed of twenty followers reaches almost no one, so for the first weeks and months she treated replying as the entire job, not a supplement to it. The targeting was deliberate. She commented on three categories of accounts: a peer in her industry whose post she could add a point of view to, her ideal audience, and her ideal audience's audience, which she found by opening a target account's follower list and picking out people who fit. She has described intentionally finding these people and then commenting on all of their posts, treating it as a process rather than a scattershot habit.

The reply itself had to earn the click. Rather than agreeing politely, she either added a unique angle or pushed back a little, telling Arvid Kahl she would "either agree but here's my also unique angle or actually kind of disagree with this, be a little bit contrarian." She admits the contrarian muscle took time to build and was not how she started, but the additive comment is what got noticed, prompted a profile visit, and converted into a follow.

She never turned the habit off. By the time Kahl looked at her Twitter profile she was nearly at 50,000 followers there, up from the roughly 2,000 she had when an early post got her trolled, and 93% of her activity was still replies. She calls detaching from commenting once you gain traction "a killer." In a separate interview she extended the same rule to LinkedIn, saying you should spend a lot of your time commenting on your pillars because those people become the ones who advocate for you and comment back.

## Lesson for Creators

An account with no followers has no distribution of its own, so the fastest path to attention is to borrow someone else's by adding value in their comments. Copy the targeting, not just the activity: list specific peers, ideal buyers, and the followers of accounts your buyers already follow, then reply on their posts with a genuine additive or contrarian take, every day, before worrying about your own posting volume. Keep replying after it starts working, because the moment you stop showing up in comments people stop treating you as present.
