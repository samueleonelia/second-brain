# A Tweet Every Day to Stay Findable

## The Story

When Jakob Greenfeld left theoretical physics for entrepreneurship, he did not just copy Pieter Levels's 12 startups in 12 months challenge. He wrapped it in a self-designed curriculum he called the Bootstrap MBA, and the curriculum included homework. As he told the Indie MBA audience, he "gave myself homework assignments like writing down 10 ideas every day", and on the Louis and Kyle Show he described the visibility half of the homework: of the assignments he set himself, "one of them was actually to put out a tweet every day". The point was never virality for its own sake. It was getting his name, face and ideas circulating while he built.

His explanation is the luck surface area model: there are proactive things you can do so that more good fortune can find you. Viral threads help because they make new people aware you exist, but Jakob puts equal weight on plain consistency, because of how memory works. People send opportunities to whoever is top of mind. He sees the inverse every time he pauses: when he stops posting and stops sending his newsletter, he fades from people's minds and, in his words, "I get fewer inbound emails and messages". Visibility is a decaying asset that daily posting keeps refreshed.

The second half of the mechanism is removing friction for the opportunities that do come. Jakob is deliberately reachable: he argues for "having your email address easy to find" on your site and to "have your Twitter DMS open", and says he is always shocked when people hide their contact route, since it silently discards the inbound their content earned. He adds one more component people rarely discuss, the setup: a website that explains who you are, a public record of what you have shipped. Every time he launches something, interesting people appear in his inbox proposing collaborations, which only works because launching, posting and reachability are all pointed at the same goal.

The badge-driven My First Million shoutout that brought him hundreds of new Twitter followers and newsletter subscribers (documented in his badge essay) is this system paying off end to end: do public work daily, sign it, and be trivially easy to contact when someone big notices.

## Lesson for Creators

Audience growth is not only push (content performing well) but pull (being findable and remembered when someone has an opportunity to hand out). The replicable play is to make visibility a scheduled obligation rather than a mood: one public post per day as non-negotiable homework, contact details one click away, DMs open. Consistency does not just grow the follower count, it keeps you in the mental index people consult when they decide who to recommend, invite or buy from.
