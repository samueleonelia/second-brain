# Five Bonus Comments on Your Own Post

## The Story

The comment section under his own post is a second piece of real estate, and Tom Alder does not leave it nearly empty. Rather than one self-reply, he stacks roughly five strong comments beneath the post. One of them is a bonus tip: he writes BONUS TIP in caps using Unicode bold characters, led by a brain or rocket emoji so it stands out, then delivers a genuinely useful extra point. Each of these comments closes with its own call to action, sometimes a subscribe prompt, sometimes a repost-it-if-you-liked-this ask.

It is the same principle as his first-hour rule applied to the comments: do not set and forget. Every comment is another surface that adds value and carries an invitation.

_"you don't just leave one comment, you leave five awesome comments on your own posts"_

## Lesson for Creators

Treat your own comment section as bonus content, not an afterthought. A handful of high-value self-comments, each with a distinct CTA, multiplies the chances a reader takes the next step.
