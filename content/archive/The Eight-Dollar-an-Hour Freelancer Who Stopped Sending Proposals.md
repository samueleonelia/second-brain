# The Eight-Dollar-an-Hour Freelancer Who Stopped Sending Proposals

## The Story

Jasmin's online business journey began in 2010, when he was a 20-year-old university student looking for extra money. He started writing 250-word articles for $8 an hour (Source 1). In his early months, he earned approximately $300. Within months, he reached about $1,000 per month (Source 1).

The turning point came when he discovered Chris Do's "The Futur" YouTube channel. He transitioned from general copywriting to brand strategy, and raised his hourly rate from $50 to $100 to $200 within a single year (Source 1).

In January 2022, he was invited to join Upwork's Expert-Vetted program, reserved for the top 1% of freelancers globally (Source 1). His current rates: website copywriting $2,000-$10,000, a single Facebook/Instagram ad costs hundreds of dollars, and consultation minimum is $500/hour (Source 1).

"I have not sent a single proposal" in seven years. All clients come through inbound referrals and word-of-mouth (Source 2). He maintains a waiting list (Source 1).

## Lesson for Creators

The path from $8/hour to $500/hour wasn't a single leap. It was a series of repositioning moves: from article writer to copywriter to brand strategist. Each shift changed not just the rate but the type of client willing to pay it. The key inflection point was learning from Chris Do and reframing his skill as strategy rather than output. Once he stopped competing on volume and started competing on expertise, the proposals became unnecessary. The work found him.
