# Outbound Comments on Big Creators

## The Story

Before he posts anything of his own, Tom Alder spends about half an hour commenting on other people's posts, specifically the large creators in his niche. He frames it bluntly as warming up the algorithm, but the real engine is arithmetic. A big creator with 500,000 followers can pull a million impressions on one post. A conservative quarter of the people who see it scroll down to read the first comment. If his comment is early and good enough to get bumped to the top, it rides that traffic.

Run 15 to 20 of those a day and one will reliably land in the top slot, putting his name in front of a quarter-million people who never saw his own posts. A small fraction click to his profile, a third of those follow. For an account that otherwise gets 500 to 1,000 impressions a post, borrowing a big account's reach is far more efficient than posting into the void.

_"for roughly half an hour before I post I would highly recommend posting comments on other people's post, particularly larger creators in your niche"_

## Lesson for Creators

Early on, other people's audiences are larger than yours, so go where they already are. A daily quota of high-value comments on big accounts in your niche is a distribution channel, not a courtesy.
