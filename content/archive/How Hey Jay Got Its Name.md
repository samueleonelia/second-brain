# How Hey Jay Got Its Name

## The Story

The name "Hey Jay" was never planned. Jasmin's clients kept misspelling his name "Jasmin," so he started signing his emails with just the single letter "J" (Source 1).

Clients began responding with "Hey J," which naturally evolved into "Hey Jay" (Source 1).

"I genuinely did not come up with it. My clients did. They came up with it for me." (Source 1).

The accidental brand name stuck and became the identity for his consultancy focused on copywriting, messaging strategy, storytelling, branding, and social strategy (Source 1).

## Lesson for Creators

Some of the best brand names aren't designed in brainstorming sessions. They emerge from how people already interact with you. Jasmin's approach of signing with "J" was a practical fix for a spelling problem, not a branding move. But his clients turned it into something memorable. The lesson: pay attention to how your audience already refers to you. Your brand might already exist in their language.
