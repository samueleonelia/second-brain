# Buying the Top of Google for Best AI Newsletter Listicles

## The Story

Adam never hired a salesperson. Every ad deal Mindstream closed in its first year came inbound, and the engineered source of that inbound was a single question. Where does an AI company looking to advertise their tool to creators actually search? The honest answer was Google, with queries like `best AI newsletters` or `advertise on AI newsletters`. Those queries returned listicle articles on third-party sites.

So Adam paid to be in the listicles.

_"We paid to get like first space on um articles of like 10 best AI newsletters"_

The maneuver had three layers. First, the paid placements. Third-party listicles that already ranked on page one accepted paid editorial slots, and Adam bought Mindstream into the top spot on every one he could. Second, the owned property. Mindstream had just acquired autogpt.net via the merger with Matitz, and that domain already had heavy SEO authority. Adam published Mindstream's own `best AI newsletters` article there and put Mindstream in the top slot. Third, the editorial wins. Forbes ran an organic listicle that placed Mindstream near the top without payment.

_"We didn't pay for Forbes. We managed to get on Forbes, but we we were kind of top spot on Google if you were searching for advertise on AI newsletters"_

A buyer searching for "advertise on AI newsletter" saw Mindstream first on three of the top results, ran out of patience, and emailed. That inbound flow is what let Adam run sales himself, lock advertisers into long-term deals at undercut CPMs, and skip the sales-team hire entirely.

## Lesson for Creators

The advertiser pipeline does not have to come from outbound. Identify the search queries an advertiser uses when they have already decided to spend money in your category, find the listicles that rank for those queries, and pay for the top slot. Better yet, own a domain in the category and publish your own version. Three top-of-page results pointing at you converts cold buyer intent into a warm inbound conversation.
