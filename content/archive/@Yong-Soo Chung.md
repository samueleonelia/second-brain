# Yong-Soo Chung

Serial entrepreneur in San Francisco. Bootstrapped a "Personal Holding Company" of four unrelated businesses ($0 → $20M, 2015–2023) — Urban EDC (everyday carry e-commerce), Spotted By Humphrey (pet brand), GrowthJet (climate-neutral 3PL), First Class Founders (podcast/newsletter). His public arc inverts the typical creator path: he operated for 8 years before going public, on the thesis that the conversation about building 8-figure businesses online was dominated by people who hadn't actually built one.
