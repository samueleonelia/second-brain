# Strip the Morning Routine to Ship the Book

## The Story

When Maja Voje sat down to write GTM Strategist, she was a consultant with a packed client calendar and a morning routine that ate the most productive part of her day. She read for an hour, walked and trained her dogs for an hour, then exercised for another hour. Three hours gone before any real work started.

Watching Alex Hormozi say he writes first thing every day, she rebuilt the morning around the one task that nobody else could do for her. "I literally stripped down my morning routine" and moved straight to the book at 6am, Mozart on, before email could hijack her attention.

The reframe underneath the schedule change was that the book was a maker's project, not a manager's. It would not get done in the gaps between meetings. As she put it, "no great thing ever in the history of humanity was was done in 30-minute breaks before the meetings." She protected a year of those mornings and shipped a best-selling book while still consulting.

## Lesson for Creators

A second product does not get built in leftover time. Identify the one task only you can do, give it the first and freshest hours of the day, and demote everything else in the routine until it ships.
