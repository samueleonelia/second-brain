# Notes as the Number One Subscriber Engine

## The Story

Olivia Wickstrom's first burst of growth came from another writer's recommendation, and when that well ran dry her chart flatlined. Instead of waiting for another lucky boost, she pivoted hard into Substack Notes. For four to five months the Notes went nowhere, then one broke through and the rest followed.

_"In the last 90 days, Notes have been my number one source of subscribers"_, she wrote, with 5,676 of them from Notes alone and a single note responsible for 2,300. Her framing of why it works is the operational part: the overwhelming majority of people seeing a Note are not yet subscribers, so she treats Notes as a constant reintroduction rather than a broadcast to existing readers.

## Lesson for Creators

Notes are built-in discovery on a platform that wants you found. Post them daily, assume each viewer is meeting you for the first time, and reintroduce yourself relentlessly instead of talking only to the people already on your list.
