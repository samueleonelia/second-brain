# Dave Kline

Dave Kline spent about a decade at Bridgewater Associates (working directly for Ray Dalio for a stretch) plus years at Moody's and PwC before leaving corporate leadership to build a management-training business with his wife. Together they run MGMT (the MGMT Accelerator cohort course and the MGMT Playbook newsletter, which lists 50,000+ subscribers and 175+ weekly issues), turning two decades of operating experience into practical playbooks for new and mid-level managers. His content spans LinkedIn and Twitter/X, feeding a newsletter, cohort courses, and one-to-one CEO coaching.

- [[The Inverted One-on-One]] -- flip 1:1s so the report owns a fixed-question agenda, turning you from status-taker into coach
- [[Always Be Recruiting]] -- continuous recruiting (a warm, pre-vetted nurture list) beats reactive hiring of whoever's available
- [[Buy Cash Flow, Skip the Big Idea]] -- buy an existing profitable audience asset for runway, then evolve it into your own product
- [[The Delegation Ladder]] -- sequence delegation: low-risk and experimental work and your weaknesses first, critical work last
- [[The I'll Just Do It Trap]] -- why taking work back destroys the team, and refusing the "monkey" with "what do you propose"
- [[Align on the How, Not Just the What]] -- co-author the system and experiments, not just the goal number, in a two-minute conversation
- [[Invest in Your High Performers First]] -- the math says a 10% lift on a 2x performer beats rescuing a 50% performer
- [[The Creator Funnel Built by Two]] -- social to newsletter to cohort course to coaching, with a husband-wife duo as the positioning feature
