# Run the Play 300 Times Before Selling the Template

## The Story

One of Maja's most popular assets is the GTM Power Hour: a template and video tutorial for listing your go-to-market assumptions and a plan to validate them in under sixty minutes.

What makes the template credible is the mileage behind it. "I have done it 300 times on a whiteboard before I published this template," she wrote. The product is the residue of a service she had already delivered hundreds of times with clients.

That sequence, do the work live many times, then productize the pattern, is why the template lands as authority rather than another framework. The reps came first and the downloadable came second.

## Lesson for Creators

Earn the right to sell a template by running the exercise live until the pattern is obvious. Productize the process only after enough reps that the artifact carries real authority, not just theory.
