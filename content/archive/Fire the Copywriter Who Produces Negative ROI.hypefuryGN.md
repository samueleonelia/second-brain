# Fire the Copywriter Who Produces Negative ROI

## The Story

Outsourcing the launch copy did not go smoothly. "For my book launch, I literally fired six copyriters" before she found something that worked.

Maja was careful to say this was not an ego clash about nobody capturing her voice. The reason was colder and simpler: the copy was not converting, and she gave each writer about a month to prove it could. "I will not pay for somebody that produces negative ROI."

The mechanism is a hard ROI gate on outsourced work. Instead of keeping a vendor out of politeness or sunk cost, she measured each one against conversion within a fixed window and cut anyone in the red. Six cuts later she had copy that paid for itself.

## Lesson for Creators

Outsourced creative earns its seat by the numbers. Set a short window, measure conversion, and replace any vendor whose work runs a negative return instead of waiting for them to grow into it.
