Differentiated in a category of 1,000+ AI-summary newsletters with a "written by humans" editorial tag, and grew through a disciplined LinkedIn engagement loop, paid-acquisition plays (Beehiiv Boost, ranked listicle placements), an equity-for-list merge, and aggressive list hygiene.
