# Master One Distribution Channel First

## The Story

In an August 2023 essay Jakob Greenfeld distills a pattern from studying serial entrepreneurs: "Every successful serial entrepreneur has mastered exactly one distribution channel." His examples: Elon Musk mastered PR, Pieter Levels mastered Twitter, Lori Grainer mastered TV, Nikita Bier mastered virality. Each of them evaluates every opportunity through the lens of that one channel, and only takes on projects the channel can carry.

The reason this works is diagnostic clarity. Entrepreneurship is drowning in uncertainty, but "if you master one distribution channel, you can at least take that part out of the equation". Founders who build product-first and distribute later can never tell why a project stalls: bad product, or bad distribution? With a mastered channel the ambiguity disappears; if it is not working, it is the product, so you can iterate fast or kill the project without wasted months. The channel also becomes a filter that makes saying no easy: if it cannot be distributed through your channel, you do not even think about it.

The acquisition path Jakob proposes is the unusual part: reverse the usual order and choose the channel before looking for opportunities, then learn it on someone else's budget. Start an agency: "You'll get paid to learn how to distribute products." Once the channel is mastered, use it to test how the market responds to your own products. He closes with the observation that "1st-time founders obsess about product, 2nd-time founders obsess about distribution", and there is no reason to wait for the second attempt to obsess about distribution from day one.

## Lesson for Creators

Audience and distribution skills compound across projects in a way individual products do not. Committing to one channel until mastery turns every future launch into a controlled experiment where only the product varies, and the agency route means the learning phase pays you instead of costing you. The filter matters as much as the skill: a mastered channel tells you which ideas to refuse.
