# LinkedIn Changed His Life in Two Years

## The Story

Jasmin Alic started actively posting on LinkedIn on January 1, 2022, with zero social media presence (Source 2). In less than two years, he accumulated over 140,000 followers (Source 2). By November 2023, when he wrote his retrospective post, he had 359,006 followers (Source 1). By 2025, the count exceeded 340,000 (Source 4).

He was ranked #1 LinkedIn creator globally by Favikon for three consecutive years, 2023 through 2025 (Source 4). He consistently receives 1,000 to 2,000 comments per post through genuine community interaction, not automation pods (Source 3).

The transformation in two years (Source 1): he transitioned to 100% remote work, increased international travel, delivered keynotes worldwide, conducted workshops across multiple countries, hosted friends and clients in Bosnia, created a new lifestyle for his family, and received opportunities he described as "previously unattainable."

During his visit to Belgrade that week, he was meeting three LinkedIn connections in person for the first time and had "just signed a new partnership deal" (Source 1).

"Write a post today, my friend. It may just change your entire life. It may just show you the entire world." (Source 1). The post received 1,455 reactions and 1,209 comments.

His daily LinkedIn routine: a dedicated "LinkedIn hour" from 12:30 PM to 1:30 PM, plus additional 15-minute commenting sessions in the morning and throughout the day (Source 3).

## Lesson for Creators

Two years. That's the timeline from zero followers to #1 global creator on LinkedIn. But the compressed timeline hides the decade of writing experience Jasmin brought to the platform. He wasn't starting from scratch in skill, just in audience. The combination of proven writing ability, daily consistency, and a structured one-hour daily commitment turned LinkedIn into the distribution channel for everything else: keynotes, workshops, coaching, and agency work. The platform was the accelerant; the skill was the fuel.
