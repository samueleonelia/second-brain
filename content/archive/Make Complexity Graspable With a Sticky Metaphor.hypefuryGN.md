# Make Complexity Graspable With a Sticky Metaphor

## The Story

Explaining why some countries thrive and others stay stuck, Pueyo splits the problem into a reusable metaphor: geography is the hardware, and institutions and culture are the software. On Infinite Loops he says of the United States that "that's the hardware, but also the software is one of the best that we've come up with so far," and he reuses the pairing across the conversation. The host reacts immediately that he wants to steal the distinction.

That reaction is the mechanism in miniature. A sticky two-part frame does the explaining, and because it is memorable, listeners repeat it. The metaphor is a comprehension tool and a distribution tool at the same time.

## Lesson for Creators

Do not just simplify; package the simplification into a phrase people can carry. A clean, reusable metaphor makes a hard idea click, and it hands readers a handle they pass on for you.
