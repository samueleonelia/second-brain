# Turn Paid On at 300 to 500 Subscribers

## The Story

Olivia Wickstrom turned on paid subscriptions almost immediately and watched it sit dead for months: 17 paid by April, 24 by June. Her correction, learned the hard way, is a specific threshold. _"I would turn on paid subscriptions somewhere between 300 and 500 free subscribers"_, not before.

The reasoning is about warmth, not vanity metrics. By a few hundred subscribers, people have read enough to trust your voice and consistency, so when you invite them to go deeper a small percentage say yes. Ask at 50 and you are asking strangers to invest in something they barely know yet.

## Lesson for Creators

Switching on paid is an ask, and asks need a warm room. Wait until a few hundred people have chosen to keep reading you, then make the offer to people who already trust the work.
