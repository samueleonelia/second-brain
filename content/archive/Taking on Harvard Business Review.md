# Taking on Harvard Business Review

## The Story

In June 2024, Alex Garcia appeared on the Blueprint podcast (Episode 25) where he laid out his vision for Marketing Examined: building a 9-figure media company that competes with Harvard Business Review (Source 1).

At that point, Marketing Examined had over 200,000 subscribers across 5 newsletters and was generating 7 figures annually (Source 3). Garcia charges $750 per hour for consulting through MentorPass and has completed 5 sessions with a 5.0/5 rating (Source 2).

The podcast covered brand strategy, creator playbooks, the vision for Marketing Examined, the playbook behind the company, and challenges facing the business (Source 1).

Garcia co-hosts the Sweat Equity podcast with Brian Blum, where they discuss the biggest marketing moves of the week covering influencer marketing, growth marketing, and content marketing (Source 3).

## Lesson for Creators

The 9-figure aspiration is notable because it reframes what a newsletter can become. Garcia isn't positioning Marketing Examined as a newsletter business. He's positioning it as a media company that happens to include newsletters. The HBR comparison sets the competitive frame: not other newsletters, but legacy business media. Whether or not he reaches 9 figures, the framing itself shapes every hiring, product, and content decision.
