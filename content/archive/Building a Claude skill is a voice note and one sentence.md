# Building a Claude skill is a voice note and one sentence

## Idea
The entire process of making a Claude skill is: open the phone app, voice-dump everything you know, and end with "use the skill creator skill" - the formatting is automatic.

## Notes & Verified Quotes
> QUOTE: "He just makes a voice note about something he knows and then with one prompt he turns it into a skill" — src: scratch/sources/claude-skills-side-hustle.txt | source: https://youtu.be/quYKZushRPo t=80
> QUOTE: "voice transcribe, brain dump all of your thoughts onto this" — src: scratch/sources/claude-skills-side-hustle.txt | source: https://youtu.be/quYKZushRPo t=1836
> QUOTE: "So it makes sure it creates it in the correct format and boom, you have a skill." — src: scratch/sources/claude-skills-side-hustle.txt | source: https://youtu.be/quYKZushRPo t=1841
> QUOTE: "you don't just want to plug and play. You want to give it your context, your instructions, your style guides" — src: scratch/sources/claude-skills-side-hustle.txt | source: https://youtu.be/quYKZushRPo t=2845

- Source: Ryan Dozer on The Kerner Office (TKO Pod). His literal workflow: pull up the Claude app, voice-transcribe a brain-dump of what he knows, and append "use the skill creator skill at the end." The skill-creator handles the structure; he doesn't format anything by hand.
- The friction that used to stop non-technical people (knowing the format, writing it cleanly) is removed: you talk, the meta-tool formats.
- Important second half: the raw skill is a starting point, not a finished one. You then "give it your context, your instructions, your style guides" so the output sounds like you, not the person whose template you borrowed.
- Angle (mine): the unlock isn't the AI being smarter, it's the *input being lazier*. The reason "everyone can do this" is true is that the capture step collapsed to talking out loud. The bottleneck was never the idea; it was the activation energy of writing it down in the right shape. Voice removes that.
- Caveat: a voice brain-dump is unstructured and repetitive; the skill-creator can only organize what you actually said. Garbage in still applies. The quality ceiling is your thinking, not the transcription.
