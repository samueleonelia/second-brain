# Thomas Shields

Thomas Shields is the founder of The Dink, a pickleball newsletter launched in November 2020 that grew into a media company reaching 750K+ pickleball players weekly across a newsletter (150K+ subscribers), two podcasts, and social media. Built while completing his MBA at UT Austin's McCombs School of Business. He is also founder of Upswing Sports, a newsletter-first parent media company expanding into emerging sports markets (F1, disc golf, esports, cycling, padel). A counter-example to the personal-brand creator pattern: The Dink is media-company-as-creator. His tactics include the Facebook-group friend-request acquisition strategy that brought the first 5,000 subscribers, a PPA Tour partnership and branded sprinter van for first-party content, a sponsor-funded referral coalition (Fromuth, Selkirk, others), and The Dink Awards (a People's Choice for pickleball) pulling in major sports orgs as co-promoters. Mark Cuban, Kevin Durant, and Jamie Foxx have all entered his orbit. Upswing Sports operates at over 50% profit margins.

## Key Patterns

- Niche-media-as-creator beats personal-brand-creator when the niche has no incumbent media coverage
- The positioning ("ESPN of pickleball") preceded the audience and became self-fulfilling
- Industry orgs are persuadable into co-promotion if you give them something they want (awards, content access, reward sponsorship)
- First-party content access (the PPA partnership + van) is the asset most newsletter-only creators lack
- Brand partnerships that fund subscriber rewards extract growth without burning newsletter margin or trust
