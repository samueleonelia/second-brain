# Matt Johansen

Matt Johansen is a cybersecurity practitioner of ~18 years who has worked at WhiteHat Security, Honest Dollar (acquired by Goldman Sachs), Bank of America, and Reddit (where he is Principal Security Architect / Head of Software Security). In 2023 he launched Vulnerable U, a free weekly cybersecurity newsletter that grew from 12 to ~30K subscribers in under two years on Beehiiv. By 2026 it had become a six-figure business with a 25-person agency arm; he left Bank of America to go full-time. He runs a five-platform stack (Newsletter, X, Instagram ~131K, TikTok, YouTube ~~ Plus LinkedIn) with ~270K-300K followers combined, and uses the brand to talk openly about mental health in the security industry — the project's name is deliberate.

## Key Patterns

- He treats each platform as its own business with its own job, not as a repurposing funnel
- He compounded "industry clout" for ~15 years before monetizing — then converted a dormant audience instantly
- Consistency is his single-most-cited growth lever: never missed a weekly issue
- Cross-domain framing (cyber frameworks → mental health) is his most repeatable content mechanic
- He rejects single-point-of-failure team design and personal-brand design alike
