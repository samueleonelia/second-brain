# Widening the Attack Surface

## The Story

Adam Robinson does not treat founder-led content as a solo act. His co-founder Santos posts in lockstep with him, and Adam actively pushes employees like Pete and Rob Clark to build their own audiences. The logic is mechanical: more people posting means more surfaces feeding the same company. As he puts it, having more people post _"widens the attack surface"_ (Maja Voje, GTM Strategist Podcast), and every one of those profiles carries the "view my website" link, which he notes is RB2B's only source of traffic.

The hard part is not the tactic but the objection every founder raises: if you build your people into public figures, won't they get poached? Adam's answer is that he considers people getting poached from him a good outcome, and he has engineered his pitch around it. His view of talent is that nothing makes someone more likely to join you than knowing that two or three years later they will be able to get a much better job because of what they learned with you. Posting is that learnable, portable asset. He calls it _"it's a forever skill"_ (Maja Voje, GTM Strategist Podcast), and because his team demonstrably knows how to do it, he can credibly promise to teach it. The company benefits a little from each person's reach; the person benefits a lot from owning the skill for life.

He is careful about how it is imposed. Building a personal profile is roughly ten times easier than building a company profile, because people follow people, not logos, so the leverage is real, but he keeps it a strong recommendation, not a requirement. If an existing employee doesn't want to use their personal profile, that's their call and their loss; the only exception is a role where posting is the job, in which case a reluctant candidate simply isn't hired.

## Lesson for Creators

One founder posting is a channel; a team posting is a network. Turn distribution into a company-wide surface by getting multiple people to post from personal profiles that all point back to you. Defuse the poaching fear by reframing: the skill you give employees is portable and career-making, which is exactly why the best people will want to join, and why leading by example beats mandating.
