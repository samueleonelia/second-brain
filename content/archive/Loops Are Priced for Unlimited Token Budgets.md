# Loops Are Priced for Unlimited Token Budgets

Ras Mic points out that the loudest advocates of autonomous agent loops, Boris and Peter, "come from a place where they have no token budgets." As evidence he cites Peter's tweet about burning $1.3 million worth of tokens in a single month. Their position is rational: they need to experiment with self-healing agents, and tokens cost them nothing. Mic says he would do the same with unlimited tokens.

The problem is the advice cascading down to people on $20 or $100 monthly plans, where a single fire-and-forget loop can consume the whole budget for slot-machine odds of a usable result. Greg names the mechanism: loops without human checkpoints are a slot machine. The practical rule that falls out: workflow advice from practitioners inherits their cost structure, so before copying a workflow, check whether you share the budget that makes it rational. Loops on a retail plan only pay off for throwaway prototypes where any outcome is acceptable.
