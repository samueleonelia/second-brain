# Content Pillars Before You Post Anything

## The Story

Before a single Note goes out, Olivia Wickstrom defines her pillars: the three or four themes everything she makes ladders up to. For Petal + Hearth those are life as an American in France, creative business ownership, and what she calls main character energy.

The rule is strict. _"every note you post should tie back to one of them"_, which sounds limiting but does the opposite. The pillars decide what she says no to, they kill the blank-screen panic, and they tell a new reader exactly what they are signing up for. She frames the counterintuitive part plainly: boundaries create freedom.

## Lesson for Creators

Pick three or four specific pillars before you chase tactics. The constraint is what makes you consistent and legible, so a stranger can tell in one scroll what your publication is actually about.
