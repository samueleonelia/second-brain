# Everyone Says Loop, Nobody Defines It

Matt Van Horn's X article "WTF Is a Loop? Peter Steinberger vs. Boris Cherny" opens with an observation about AI-coding discourse: "The most repeated sentence in AI coding this week is six words long, and almost nobody saying it can define it." The article contrasts how Peter Steinberger and Boris Cherny each mean different things by running agents "in a loop," after one tweet "had the entire timeline in a chokehold."

Even from the preview, the meta-point stands on its own: AI-coding vocabulary spreads faster than its meaning. A term coined by practitioners with a precise workflow behind it gets repeated as a slogan, and within a week the same word covers incompatible practices. When two named experts disagree on what a core term means, copying the term without copying the underlying workflow copies nothing.
