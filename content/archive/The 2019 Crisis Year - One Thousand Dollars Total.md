# The 2019 Crisis Year: One Thousand Dollars Total

## The Story

In 2019, Jasmin Alic earned only $1,000 for the entire year (Source 1). After years of building a freelance copywriting career, his income collapsed to nearly nothing.

He responded by pivoting. He created "Creative Maze," a brand selling posters and gifts. The Facebook page reached 10,000 followers in four months (Source 1). The venture represented a survival attempt during what would become the lowest financial point of his career.

This crisis year fell between his teaching career and his explosive LinkedIn growth that began in January 2022. The man who would later charge $500/hour for consultations and work with Microsoft and Marriott Bonvoy spent an entire year earning less than two months of his old teaching salary.

## Lesson for Creators

The timeline matters. Jasmin's origin story is often compressed into a clean arc: rapper, teacher, freelancer, LinkedIn star. The 2019 gap, where he earned almost nothing and tried selling posters to survive, is the part that usually gets skipped. But it's the most instructive part. A near-zero year doesn't disqualify you from a seven-figure future. It just means the path wasn't linear, and it rarely is.
