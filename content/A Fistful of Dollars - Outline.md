
## **Setup**

1. A lone stranger rides into San Miguel.  
    **Point:** Introduce the outsider.
2. The town is dusty, poor, and silent.  
    **Point:** Establish moral emptiness.
3. A child sneak in a house and he get chased out by men who shoot at him running away crying
    **Point:** Show innocent suffering.
4. Men threaten and kick the child's father
    **Point:** Show the town is ruled by cruelty.
5. The stranger notices everything. He stays out of it, but his presence stops the beating.
    **Point:** He is quiet, observant, dangerous.
6. The stranger crosses the street of a man being banned by the city
	**Point:** Foreigners are not welcome
7. A bell-ringer tells him the town is a place for dead people or killer.
    **Point:** Anticipate there will be blood
8. Baxter's men shoot at the stranger
	**Point:** The game begins
9. The stranger meet the barman and learn about the town. San Miguel is controlled by two rival families.  
    **Point:** Define the conflict.
10. The Baxters control one side.  
    **Point:** One power bloc.
11. The Rojos control the other. They are the strongest thanks to Ramo
    **Point:** The more violent power bloc and main antagonist
12. The stranger realizes both sides need gunmen.  
    **Point:** He sees opportunity.

---

## **The Stranger Enters the Game**

13. He tells the undertaker to prepare coffins.  
    **Point:** Establish confidence.
14. He kills the thugs quickly.  
    **Point:** Prove his skill.
15. The town now fears him.  
    **Point:** Shift his status.
16. He sells his services to the Rojos.  
    **Point:** Begins playing one side.
17. He learns the Rojos are ambitious criminals, and they are already planning to kill him at the first opportunity
    **Point:** Every one plan its own double crossing
18. The stranger moves back to the bar
	**Point:** Friendship with the barman starts growing


---

## **The Central Scheme Begins**

20. A shipment of gold arrives in the city guarded by soldiers.  
    **Point:** Introduce valuable stakes.
21. Ramón massacres the soldiers.  
    **Point:** Show his ruthlessness.
22. The Rojos disguise the massacre as an attack by Americans.  
    **Point:** Show they manipulate politics too.
23. The stranger secretly witnesses the massacre.  
    **Point:** He gains leverage.
24. He sees that both families can be tricked.  
    **Point:** The plot becomes a con game.
25. He moves dead soldiers’ bodies to a cemetery.  
    **Point:** Creates false evidence.
26. He tells both families survivors are hiding there.  
    **Point:** Sets them against each other.
27. Both sides rush to capture the “survivors.”  
    **Point:** They expose their greed and fear.
28. The stranger watches them fight.  
    **Point:** He profits from chaos.
29. His strategy becomes clear: make both sides destroy each other.  
    **Point:** Main plot engine.

---

## **Marisol**

31. The stranger sees Marisol.  
    **Point:** Introduce emotional center.
32. Marisol is kept by Ramón.  
    **Point:** Show Ramón’s private evil.
33. She is separated from her husband and son.  
    **Point:** Give the story innocent victims.
34. Ramón “won” her through violence and deceit.  
    **Point:** Make his villainy personal.
35. The stranger notices her pain.  
    **Point:** Reveal he is not only greedy.
36. Marisol becomes the moral test.  
    **Point:** The stranger must choose between money and justice.
37. He asks about her.  
    **Point:** He begins caring.
38. He learns her family is still alive.  
    **Point:** Create a possible rescue.
39. Ramón treats Marisol as property.  
    **Point:** Clarify what must be opposed.
40. The stranger’s plan changes.  
    **Point:** He now has a personal mission.

---

## **Playing Both Sides**

41. The stranger keeps switching loyalties.  
    **Point:** Maintain tension.
42. He takes money from the Rojos.  
    **Point:** Uses greed against them.
43. He takes money from the Baxters.  
    **Point:** Uses desperation against them.
44. Neither side fully trusts him.  
    **Point:** Keep danger constant.
45. Both sides still need him.  
    **Point:** Explain why he survives.
46. He provokes paranoia.  
    **Point:** The families weaken themselves.
47. He makes small lies look like big truths.  
    **Point:** His weapon is manipulation.
48. The town becomes more unstable.  
    **Point:** His strategy is working.
49. The undertaker profits from deaths.  
    **Point:** Dark comic commentary.
50. The innkeeper warns him repeatedly.  
    **Point:** Voice of caution.

---

## **Rescue of Marisol**

51. The stranger attacks Ramón’s guards.  
    **Point:** First major selfless act.
52. He frees Marisol.  
    **Point:** Moves from schemer to rescuer.
53. He reunites her with her husband and son.  
    **Point:** Gives the story emotional payoff.
54. He gives them money.  
    **Point:** Confirms he is not driven only by profit.
55. He tells them to escape.  
    **Point:** Protects innocence from the town.
56. Marisol asks why he helps.  
    **Point:** Reveal hidden past.
57. He says he once knew someone like her.  
    **Point:** Suggest backstory without explaining it.
58. The family flees.  
    **Point:** One good thing escapes the corruption.
59. The stranger stays behind.  
    **Point:** He accepts the consequences.
60. His deception is discovered.  
    **Point:** The plot turns against him.

---

## **The Stranger Falls**

61. Ramón realizes the stranger betrayed him.  
    **Point:** The villain catches up.
62. The Rojos capture the stranger.  
    **Point:** Remove his control.
63. They beat him brutally.  
    **Point:** Strip away his invincible image.
64. Ramón wants Marisol back.  
    **Point:** His obsession drives the next escalation.
65. The stranger refuses to reveal her location.  
    **Point:** He chooses honor over survival.
66. He escapes badly wounded.  
    **Point:** Keeps him alive but weakened.
67. He crawls through town.  
    **Point:** Show physical cost.
68. The coffin-maker helps hide him.  
    **Point:** The “death” imagery becomes survival.
69. He is smuggled out in a coffin.  
    **Point:** Symbolic rebirth.
70. The Rojos think he is gone.  
    **Point:** Give him time to recover.

---

## **The Rojos Take Over**

71. The Rojos attack the Baxters.  
    **Point:** The town war reaches its peak.
72. They burn the Baxter house.  
    **Point:** Show total destruction.
73. The Baxters are slaughtered.  
    **Point:** One faction is eliminated.
74. The sheriff is powerless.  
    **Point:** Law is meaningless.
75. Ramón becomes the town’s ruler.  
    **Point:** The worst side wins temporarily.
76. The stranger hears what happened.  
    **Point:** Raises urgency.
77. His earlier game has created a monster’s victory.  
    **Point:** His manipulation has consequences.
78. He prepares to return.  
    **Point:** The final confrontation begins forming.
79. He builds armor under his poncho.  
    **Point:** Counter Ramón’s rifle advantage.
80. He chooses direct action over trickery.  
    **Point:** Character shift.

---

## **Final Showdown**

81. The stranger walks back into town.  
    **Point:** Mythic return.
82. Ramón is shocked to see him alive.  
    **Point:** Reverse the power dynamic.
83. Ramón shoots him in the chest.  
    **Point:** Test the hidden armor.
84. The stranger keeps standing.  
    **Point:** Creates fear and mystery.
85. Ramón shoots again and again.  
    **Point:** His confidence collapses.
86. The stranger advances slowly.  
    **Point:** Turn him into a symbol of judgment.
87. He reveals the metal plate.  
    **Point:** Explain the trick.
88. He challenges Ramón pistol against rifle.  
    **Point:** Make the duel personal.
89. Ramón reaches for his weapon.  
    **Point:** Final contest.
90. The stranger kills Ramón.  
    **Point:** Defeat the main evil.
91. He kills the remaining Rojos.  
    **Point:** Finish the cleansing of the town.
92. Silence returns.  
    **Point:** The violence is over.

---

## **Resolution**

93. The town is free of both families.  
    **Point:** The stranger’s original goal is achieved.
94. The innocent family has escaped.  
    **Point:** The moral goal is achieved.
95. The innkeeper survives.  
    **Point:** A decent local remains.
96. The undertaker has more work.  
    **Point:** Dark comic ending.
97. The stranger refuses to stay.  
    **Point:** He belongs nowhere.
98. He collects no grand reward.  
    **Point:** He is not a normal hero.
99. He rides away alone.  
    **Point:** Restore the lone-wanderer myth.
100. San Miguel is changed, but he is not.  
    **Point:** He solves the town’s problem, then disappears.
